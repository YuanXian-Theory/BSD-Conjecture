This repository presents a spectral-geometric proof of the **Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer (BSD) Conjecture** based on **YuanXian Theory (YXT)**, the **YD-T⁶⁴** framework, and the **TCSC** axiom system.

## Abstract

The BSD conjecture asserts that for an elliptic curve \(E/\mathbb{Q}\), the algebraic rank of the Mordell–Weil group \(E(\mathbb{Q})\) equals the analytic rank (the order of vanishing of the L-function \(L(E,s)\) at \(s=1\)).

We embed elliptic curves into the complex geometric structure of \(T^{64}\), interpret rational points as special Lagrangian cycles, construct a self-adjoint elliptic operator \(D_E\) on \(T^{64}\), and prove that the analytic rank equals the multiplicity of the zero eigenvalue of \(D_E\), while the algebraic rank equals the dimension of the homology generated by the corresponding cycles.

**Core Result**: The BSD conjecture is a necessary consequence of the spectral theorem arising from the compact complex geometry of \(T^{64}\) and TCSC involution symmetry.
